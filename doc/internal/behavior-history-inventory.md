## Reading the ledger

“Question” means the integration contributes to a linked intent question, not that its whole diff is suspect. “Correction” restores an existing contract or fixes a bounded source defect without asking whether the defect is acceptable. “Resolved” records explicit maintainer intent. “Superseded” records a reverted/replaced intermediate change. “Feature-screened” records an additive surface without a selected unresolved policy question. “Support” covers inspected test/docs/build/metadata changes; it does not prove that a dependency or CI system has no behavioral differences.

Depth is stream-specific: deep/source-and-consumers means a scoped path was traced into relevant producers/consumers, not a full review of that merge. A screen is a path/diff assessment. All stream names below show where the integration was considered; only owning dispositions are summarized. An outside-stream/handoff record alone never satisfies coverage. Consolidation owns eight handoffs found while reconciling the queues; authentication owns its 28 supplements. Source concerns use their report-local C IDs and are not unanswered intent choices.
